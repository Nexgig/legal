Delete Your Account

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Nexgig account and the data associated with it. Nexgig ("Nexgig", "we", "us", or "our") is a booking and roster management platform that connects venue managers with artists (DJs and live performers).

Delete your account from the app

You can permanently delete your account at any time, directly inside the Nexgig app:

  1. Open the Nexgig app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. This deletes the role you are using now. If you also hold the other role (manager and artist) under the same login, that role and your login remain. If it is your last or only role, your login and profile are removed too. Either way, any upcoming bookings for the deleted role are cancelled, and some records are kept for the other party you dealt with — see "What data may be kept" below.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in to the app, email us from the address on your account at admin@nexgigapp.com with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify your request and delete your account, normally within 7 days.

What data is deleted

If you hold both a manager and an artist profile under one login, you can remove just one role; your other role and your login remain. Deleting your last (or only) role removes your login and the personal data listed here:

  • Your login and authentication details;
  • Your profile — name, stage/legal name, email address, phone number, chosen avatar, bio, location, nationality, genres, instruments, and links;
  • For venue managers: your venues and their details;
  • Your availability, roster connections, notifications, and push-notification token.

What data may be kept

Sent invoices, and the booking records needed to support them, may be kept as a business and financial record for the other party (the venue or artist you dealt with), with your personal link to them removed where possible and your name replaced on shared bookings. A retained invoice file may still contain the original names and business details captured when it was created — these files are not anonymised.

How long a retained record is kept depends on why it exists — for example, any accounting or tax obligations that apply, and whether there is an unresolved dispute between the parties. We are finalising the specific retention periods and do not keep these records indefinitely without a reason. We may also retain limited information where required to comply with legal obligations.

Questions

For anything related to your data or this process, contact us at admin@nexgigapp.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
