Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexgig ("Nexgig", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Nexgig mobile application (the "App") and the Nexgig website at nexgigapp.com (the "Site"), including the "Request access" form on the Site. Nexgig is a booking and roster management platform that connects venue managers with artists (DJs and live performers). Nexgig is operated by Elie Turk, based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For privacy requests or questions about this policy, contact admin@nexgigapp.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information that you provide or that is generated through your use of the App and the Site:

  • Account information: your email address, phone number, name (including stage name and legal name), username, and password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
  • Profile information: location (city/country), nationality, genres, instruments, bio, and the links you choose to add. For your profile picture you may either choose one of Nexgig's built-in avatars or upload a photo from your device's photo library. Uploaded photos are stored on our servers and shown to other users of the App. The App accesses your photo library only when you choose to upload a photo, and does not use your camera.
  • Venue information (managers): venue names, addresses, venue type, capacity, descriptions, and related business details. Venue imagery is either supplied by Nexgig from the venue type you select, or a photo you upload from your device's photo library; uploaded venue photos are stored on our servers and shown to other users of the App.
  • Files you upload: if you are an artist, you can create an invoice in the App or upload your own invoice file (a PDF or a photo of an invoice) instead. Files you upload are stored on our servers so the manager you are invoicing can open them (see "Your Business and Financial Information" below for how access to invoice files is restricted).
  • Booking and activity data: sets, bookings, lineup assignments, availability, invoices, and notifications generated through your use of the App.
  • Financial information: the fees you set for sets and bookings, per-venue budgets, monthly totals, and the invoices you send or receive.
  • Device information: a push notification token (if you enable notifications) used to deliver alerts to your device.
  • Diagnostic data: crash reports and error logs generated when the App encounters a problem, used to detect, diagnose, and fix issues. We configure our error reporting not to attach your name, email, or contact details; a report may include a technical account identifier so an error can be traced to the affected account.
  • Access requests (Site): if you use the "Request access" form on our website, we collect the name, email address, phone number (if provided), and the venues or events you tell us you manage. We use these details to review your request and contact you about onboarding. Submitting the form does not create a Nexgig account, does not approve you as a manager, and does not subscribe you to marketing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the App's core features (booking, roster management, scheduling, invoicing);
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • Verify venues during onboarding — confirm that a venue exists and that the manager is authorised to represent it (see "Venue Verification" in our Terms of Service);
  • Display your profile and activity to relevant managers or artists you connect with;
  • Send you notifications about bookings, requests, and account activity;
  • Review access requests and respond to support enquiries;
  • Improve and maintain the App and the Site.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

Nexgig is a two-sided platform, so some information is shared as part of normal use:

  • When you connect with a manager or artist, relevant profile and booking details are visible to that connected party.
  • We use trusted third-party service providers to operate Nexgig, which process data on our behalf under their agreements with us:
    • Supabase — database, user authentication, file storage, hosting, and serverless functions;
    • Expo — delivery of over-the-air app updates and push notifications;
    • Resend — email delivery, including invoice emails, notification emails, and access-request notifications;
    • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Nexgig and its users.

4. Your Business and Financial Information

We understand that the fees, budgets, and invoices you manage in Nexgig are commercially sensitive. This section explains who can and cannot see them.

  • Fees you set. When you offer a booking, the artist you offer it to sees the fee for their own gig — they need this to accept the booking and invoice you. Other managers and other venues do not see your fees, and an artist does not see your budgets, your other artists' fees, your monthly totals, or your reports.
  • Budgets, totals, and reports. Your per-venue budgets, monthly totals, and billing exports are shown only to you as the manager of that venue.
  • Invoices. An invoice is shared between the two parties to it. When an artist sends an invoice, it is delivered to the manager in the App and by email — to the venue's billing email address if one is set, otherwise to the manager's account email. A manager can export their own venue's billing records.
  • Uploaded invoice files are stored privately. Nexgig checks that the requester is authorised before issuing a temporary download link. Anyone holding that link may use it until it expires. Invoices may also be delivered by email to the designated billing recipient.
  • We do not sell, rent, or advertise with this information. We do not disclose your fees, budgets, totals, or reports to any other user or to any outside company for their own purposes.
  • Legal exceptions. We may disclose this information only where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Nexgig and its users.

5. Data Storage and Hosting

Your account and activity data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, with servers located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Some of our service providers (for example, email delivery and diagnostics) may process limited data in other countries in order to deliver their part of the service. By using the App and the Site, you acknowledge that your information may be processed and stored in these locations, which may be outside the United Arab Emirates.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete Account). See our account deletion page for full details. In summary:

  • Removing one role from a dual-role account. If you hold both a manager and an artist profile under one login, deleting from one side removes only that role's profile. Your other role and your login remain.
  • Deleting your full account. When you delete your last (or only) role, we remove your login, your profile details, your uploaded profile photo, your notifications, and your push-notification token.
  • Records kept for the other party. Sent invoices, and the booking records needed to support them, are kept privately as a business and financial record for the other party (the venue or artist you dealt with). Your personal link to those records is removed where possible, and your name is replaced on shared bookings. However, a retained invoice file may still contain the original names and business details captured when it was created — these files are not anonymised.
  • How long retained records are kept. How long we keep a retained invoice or booking record depends on why the record exists — for example, any accounting or tax obligations that apply, and whether there is an unresolved dispute between the parties. We are finalising the specific retention periods; we do not keep these records indefinitely without a reason.
  • Access requests. We keep an access request for up to 12 months after our last meaningful contact with you, and then delete it — unless the request has become part of an ongoing relationship with us, or we are required to keep it for a specific legal reason.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, and to object to or restrict how we use it. You can delete most of your data yourself from within the App (Settings → Delete Account). To make any other request, or if you cannot sign in, email us at admin@nexgigapp.com from the address on your account, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children's Privacy

Nexgig is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information — for example, invoice files are kept in private storage and opened through temporary links that we issue only after checking the requester is authorised. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will update the effective date above when we do. If we make a significant change — for example, collecting new types of information or using your information for a materially different purpose — we will tell you before it takes effect, through the App or by email, and we will obtain your consent where the law requires it. We do not treat your continued use of the App as consent to a new, incompatible purpose.

11. Contact Us

Nexgig is operated by Elie Turk, based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For privacy requests or questions about this policy, contact admin@nexgigapp.com.
